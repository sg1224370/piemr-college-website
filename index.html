About the Institution

About PIEMR

Prestige Institute of Engineering Management & Research (PIEMR), located in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, is committed to cultivating capable engineers and innovators through a balanced blend of foundational theory and practical technical application.

Under the Prestige Education Foundation, the institute places strong emphasis on synthesis, creativity, hands-on engineering experience, effective communication, and problem-solving grounded in real-world industrial and societal challenges.

Featured Engineering Disciplines

Academic Programs

Explore featured engineering programmes at PIEMR. These highlighted disciplines represent key areas of study and laboratory training across computing, intelligence, automation, and core infrastructure.

01 · Computing

Computer Science & Engineering

Builds strong foundations in algorithms, data structures, software engineering, operating systems, databases, and modern cloud architectures.

02 · Intelligence

AI & Data Science

Focuses on machine learning, statistical modelling, neural networks, data analytics, and intelligent decision-making systems.

03 · Industry 4.0

Automation & Robotics

Integrates embedded control, industrial automation, sensor systems, and mechatronic design for smart manufacturing environments.

04 · Infrastructure

Civil Engineering

Covers structural analysis, geotechnical studies, construction materials testing, fluid mechanics, and sustainable urban infrastructure.

05 · Power Systems

Electrical Engineering

Explores electrical machines, power generation and distribution, control systems, smart grids, and renewable energy technologies.

06 · Design & Manufacturing

Mechanical Engineering

Trains students in thermodynamics, machine design, CAD/CAM systems, manufacturing technology, and applied mechanical prototyping.

Inquiry & Technology Learning

Research and Innovation

Engineering progress relies on curiosity, structured experimentation, and the willingness to examine complex problems from first principles. At PIEMR, research and innovation are woven into undergraduate learning to strengthen critical thinking beyond textbook exercises.

Through specialized computing, machine learning, automation, and materials testing laboratories, students learn how to design experiments, analyze empirical results, and adapt to rapidly evolving technology ecosystems.

  • 01. Applied Problem Solving: Encouraging student projects that address real engineering, sustainability, and software challenges.
  • 02. Interdisciplinary Exploration: Connecting computer science, AI, electronics, and mechanical systems inside collaborative labs.
  • 03. Continuous Technology Learning: Building lifelong adaptability through virtual labs, technical workshops, and prototyping.
